CleanChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

CleanChat ("the app", "we") is made by Yuliyan Slavchev, an individual developer, ul. Sveta Gora 7, Apt 10, Sofia 1000, Bulgaria. Contact: cleanchat.support@gmail.com.

We built CleanChat to collect as little as possible. In short: we have no servers, no user accounts, no analytics and no ads. We never see your Instagram password or your messages.

1. Signing in to Instagram

CleanChat shows Instagram's own website inside the app. You sign in on Instagram's login page, so your username and password go directly to Instagram (Meta Platforms) — never to us. Instagram then keeps you signed in with cookies that are stored only on your device, inside the app. We do not copy, read, send or store them anywhere else.

Your messages and everything else you see in the app are loaded from Instagram and shown on your device. We do not receive, store or analyse them. What Instagram does with your data is covered by Instagram's own Privacy Policy.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your answers to the short onboarding quiz (your goal, age and rough screen-time estimates), used only to show you your personal numbers. They stay on your device.
  • Whether you have finished the onboarding.
  • If you allow notifications: one reminder, scheduled on your device, the day before your free trial ends.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. "Log out of Instagram" in Settings ends your Instagram session.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To check whether you have an active subscription we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives your purchase history from Apple and an anonymous app ID that is not linked to your name, email or Instagram account. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4. What we don't do

  • No tracking across apps or websites, no advertising, no selling of data.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or microphone by CleanChat itself. (If you send a photo or voice message in a chat, iOS asks you, and the file goes to Instagram.)

5. Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

We process the purchase data in section 3 to provide the subscription you bought (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can ask us about any data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to cleanchat.support@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (www.cpdp.bg) or your local authority.

6. Children

CleanChat is meant for people who are old enough to use Instagram (13+ under Instagram's terms).

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.

CleanChat is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.
