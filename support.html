Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › CleanChat › Cancel Subscription. Or in CleanChat: Settings › Manage subscription.

I bought CleanChat on another phone. Open the paywall and tap “Restore”, using the same Apple ID.

Instagram asks me for a security code. Instagram does this when you sign in on a new device. Enter the code Instagram sends you; CleanChat never sees it.

How do I log out? CleanChat › Settings › Log out of Instagram.
